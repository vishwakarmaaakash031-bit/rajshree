Aradhna 💗

Rajshree · my Aradhna

Some people walk into your life and quietly make everything brighter. That’s you. Your smile feels like sunrise, your eyes hold a whole sky of stars, and your kindness makes you even more beautiful than you look.

You are not just pretty, Aradhna. You are warm, graceful, and one of a kind. Every moment with you feels like a little piece of heaven, and I’m so lucky you’re mine.

Aradhna

Some names feel like a gentle prayer, Soft as the morning’s golden air. Aradhna, when I say your name, My whole world is never the same. You are the calm after every storm, The place my heart feels safe and warm.

Your Smile

Your smile is the first light of day, It chases all my worries away. The stars forget to shine so bright When your eyes are full of light. If I could keep one thing for good, It would be your laugh, as it should.

Forever Yours

Seasons change and years go by, But you stay the star in my sky. On every birthday, big or small, I’ll love you most of all. Rajshree, my heart’s sweet song, With you is where I belong.
